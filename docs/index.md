## Team Introduction
> Team 101 is developing an Automatic Pet Food Dispenser for the EGR 304 Embedded System Design course at Arizona State University during Fall 2026.

The goal of this project is to design an embedded system that can automatically dispense pet food using coordinated sensors, microcontrollers, and motor-control components. The system is divided into individual subsystems that work together to detect conditions, process information, and control food dispensing.

Each team member is responsible for designing, analyzing, and documenting an individual subsystem. These subsystems will be integrated to create a functional automatic pet food dispenser.

This report documents our engineering design process, including:

- **User Needs and Benchmarking:** Identifying customer needs and evaluating existing solutions.
- **Product Requirements:** Establishing measurable engineering specifications.
- **Design Ideation:** Developing and comparing possible solutions.
- **Team Block Diagram:** Illustrating the overall system architecture and subsystem connections.
- **Software Proposal:** Describing the planned embedded-system software and control logic.

Individual team member datasheets provide additional details about component selection, bills of materials, schematics, and power budgets.
