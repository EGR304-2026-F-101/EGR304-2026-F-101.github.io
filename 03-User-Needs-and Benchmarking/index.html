    Search #1

    Keywords: "Timed Medicine Dispenser"

    Search Results Link: https://www.amazon.com/timed-medicine-dispenser/s?k=timed+medicine+dispenser

    Selected Products

    1. Colorwing® 2025 Smart Pill Dispenser for Seniors

    • Price: $129.99

    • Vendor: Colorwing via Amazon

    • Description: The COLORWING Smart Pill Dispenser is an automatic medication dispenser designed to release medication at scheduled times. It uses multiple medication compartments, programmable reminders, locking features, and a dispensing tray.

    Positive Comments
    Voice of the Customer 1 Restated Customer Need
    "This case solved that problem as the segments are all individually capped and the entire box has a lid that will also close over the top of everything. We've used these about a year and they have held up well... They are easy to open and snap shut well. No issues of coming open even when container has been accidentally dropped." 1. The dispenser securely contains its contents during normal use. (explicit)
    2. The dispenser remains durable after extended use. (explicit)
    3. The dispenser is easy for the user to open and close. (explicit)
    Voice of the Customer 2 Restated Customer Need
    "Each slot opens separately which means no spilling the rest and easy to set out if assisting is necessary... There is plenty of space in each slot for pills... This has been getting daily use for almost a year and have had no issues with it at all. The markings are still crisp to read and the lids all work as if they were new!" 1. The dispenser keeps stored contents separated and organized. (explicit)
    2. The dispenser provides enough storage capacity for the intended contents. (explicit)
    3. The dispenser maintains its functionality after frequent, long-term use. (explicit)
    Voice of the Customer 3 Restated Customer Need
    "Once you’ve filled it, it’s done for a MONTH... The compartments are plenty big enough... And, they stay shut when you close them." 1. The dispenser stores enough contents for an extended period of use. (explicit)
    2. The dispenser minimizes how frequently the user needs to refill it. (latent)
    3. The dispenser keeps its contents securely contained until they are needed. (latent)
    Negative Comments
    Voice of the Customer 1 Restated Customer Need
    "The rechargeable battery doesn't last even a day, so you have to keep it connected to a wall outlet. Second of all, the door that's supposed to open and drop the pills does not open every time. The app that you're supposed to connect to to set up your reminders does not work well at all. It's very disconnected from the actual device. The instructions are marginal at best." 1. The dispenser remains operational for an adequate amount of time without external power. (explicit)
    2. The dispenser reliably releases its contents when scheduled. (explicit)
    3. The dispenser provides a simple and reliable method for setting and managing dispensing times. (latent)
    Voice of the Customer 2 Restated Customer Need
    "My unit stop automatically dispensing after 5 days. Now it stopped dispensing at all with an error message ‘unit disabled after frequent use’. There is no amount of troubleshooting you can do expect factory reset and add all of your settings back to it again." 1. The dispenser continues to operate reliably after frequent use. (explicit)
    2. The dispenser allows users to recover from errors without losing their saved settings. (latent)
    3. The dispenser is easy to troubleshoot when a malfunction occurs. (latent)
    Voice of the Customer 3 Restated Customer Need
    "Hard to open lids for pills. Hard to get pills out - you have to dump them in your hand. The daily cases don't sit on the case tightly, so they fall out and wiggle around. Plenty large enough, nice colors." 1. The dispenser is easy for the user to open and access. (explicit)
    2. The dispenser securely holds removable components in their intended positions. (latent)
    3. The dispenser accommodates an adequate amount of material without being unnecessarily large. (latent)

    • Vendor: Georgia-Pacific via Amazon

    "Works perfectly every time! Paper roll is lasting longer than expected." 1. The dispenser operates reliably. (explicit)
    2. The dispenser dispenses paper consistently. (latent)
    3. The paper supply lasts for an extended period of use. (explicit)
    Voice of the Customer 2 Restated Customer Need
    "Very sensitive sensor. Triggered at the slightest approach. Not only your hand, even your head can be put to the sensor and it will give you a piece of paper." 1. The dispenser responds to user movement. (explicit)
    2. The dispenser provides paper without requiring physical contact. (explicit)
    3. The sensor detects users from a convenient distance. (latent)
    Voice of the Customer 3 Restated Customer Need
    "I have it for two weeks, still working good." 1. The dispenser continues to function after repeated use. (explicit)
    2. The dispenser is durable during regular use. (latent)
    3. The dispenser maintains its performance over time. (latent)
    Voice of the Customer 4 Restated Customer Need
    "Great price so far so good." 1. The dispenser provides good value for its cost. (explicit)
    2. The dispenser performs as expected. (latent)
    3. The dispenser is worth purchasing. (latent)
    Negative Comments
    Voice of the Customer 1 Restated Customer Need
    "didnt work — bulky, needs brackets that are not included and didnt work right out of the box" 1. The dispenser is compact. (explicit)
    2. The dispenser includes the hardware needed for installation. (explicit)
    3. The dispenser works correctly when first installed. (explicit)
    Voice of the Customer 2 Restated Customer Need
    "This towel dispenser is quiet and works well except it will not fit other standard rolls. You can use it if you modify it..." 1. The dispenser accepts standard paper towel rolls. (explicit)
    2. The dispenser does not require modifications to use compatible rolls. (latent)
    3. The dispenser is compatible with a variety of paper towel rolls. (latent)
    Voice of the Customer 3 Restated Customer Need
    "This dispenser does not use standard 8" rolls. Do not buy unless you want to buy their specific rolls" 1. The dispenser accommodates standard-sized paper towel rolls. (explicit)
    2. Users can choose from different paper towel brands. (latent)
    3. The dispenser does not require specialized refills. (latent)
    Voice of the Customer 4 Restated Customer Need
    "These have a tough time supporting full roll. I had to rig up a hallow pipe and attach it between the holding brackets. It work fine now." 1. The dispenser securely supports a full paper towel roll. (explicit)
    2. The dispenser's roll supports are strong enough for normal use. (latent)
    3. The dispenser does not require modifications to support a full roll. (latent)

    3. PetSafe FlexFeed Automatic Pet Feeder

    • Price: $64.99

    • Vendor: PetSafe via Petco.com

    • Description: Easy to program and convenient for busy pet parents, the FlexFeed Automatic Pet Feeder helps maintain a consistent and healthy eating routine with as many as 8 meals or snacks per day for up to 2 weeks. With a dishwasher-safe, stainless-steel bowl that's easy to clean, an airtight lid to keep food fresh, and dual-power options, FlexFeed keeps your cat or small dog full, happy and flourishing.

    Positive Comments
    Voice of the Customer 1 Restated Customer Need
    "Just bought this and was impressed at how easy it was to set up. We have a Red Standard Poodle puppy and are feeding him every four hours so this is perfect. I love that I can record a message to him as well. He knows exactly when it’s going to release the food so it keeps him on his schedule perfectly." 1. The dispenser is easy to program (explicit)
    2. Extra features such as recorded messages set the product apart (explicit)
    3. Easy for pets to understand (latent)
    Voice of the Customer 2 Restated Customer Need
    "Easy to program with no app or wifi required. It just works. Construction is high quality. I’ve always been impressed with PetSafe products." 1. The product is reputable and well tested (latent)
    2. The product is well constructed and sturdy (explicit)
    3. No connection is required, closed system (explicit)
    Voice of the Customer 3 Restated Customer Need
    "his auto electronic pet feeder has been a godsend for my two tabby cats! It's literally just set it and forget it! It holds 2 bags of cat food at once and lasts all week long for multiple times a day if needed. You can set it up however you'd like to utilize it for timing of feeds, which is very convenient. It's made well and looks sleek in our laundry room as well. Much better than the unsightly bowls we had prior. I was provided this product by Influenster in exchange for my honest review. Would definitely not hesitate to purchase!" 1. The dispenser can hold a good amount of food (explicit)
    2. The dispenser requires minimal interaction (latent)
    3. The product looks good (explicit)
    Negative Comments
    Voice of the Customer 1 Restated Customer Need
    "I think the idea of this is great, but it wasn’t really necessary for my family. It took up too much space and took more time than just hand feeding my pets. It might be great if a family goes on vacation and leaves pets at home." 1. The product is compact (explicit)
    2. The product has a short dispense time (explicit)
    3. The product functions while the user is away (latent)
    Voice of the Customer 2 Restated Customer Need
    "In theory, I thought this product would be a godsend to me. In all reality, though it is convenient and appears very durable, the accuracy of setting the customized times for feeding did not work for me. I tried to set alarms and food was dispersed on off times. Maybe that’s just me, but I think it’s worth mentioning. I’ll also say for some weird reason, all 3 of my extremely food motivated dogs refused to eat out of this feeder. I don’t know what that means, if anything?" 1. The food dispenses when programmed (explicit)
    2. Pets should like the dispenser (latent)
    3. The setting changes should be accurate (explicit)
    Voice of the Customer 3 Restated Customer Need
    "The auto feeder didn’t dispense the food returning" 1. The dispenser should be able to work with different food sizes (latent)
    2. The dispenser should be user friendly (latent)
    3. Food should be dispensed when activated (explicit)
